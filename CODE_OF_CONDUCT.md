# Code of Conduct — School Guardian

**Project:** School Guardian  
**Organization:** school-guardian-project  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the principles and expected behaviors for everyone involved in the development, maintenance, administration, and collaboration of the School Guardian project.

Its purpose is to promote a respectful, professional, inclusive, and collaborative working environment where individuals can contribute to the project constructively and responsibly.

## 2. Scope

This code applies to everyone participating in School Guardian, regardless of their role, access level, or form of collaboration.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Organization owners and administrators.
- Architects and technical leads.
- Maintainers and reviewers.
- Internal and external contributors.
- Participants in discussions, issues, and pull requests.
- Individuals involved in official project activities.

This code applies to the project's official digital spaces as well as interactions related to its activities and representation.

## 3. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, courtesy, and professionalism.
- Communicate clearly, constructively, and respectfully.
- Accept differences of opinion and well-founded technical criteria.
- Provide feedback aimed at improving the project, not belittling individuals.
- Acknowledge the contributions and work of others.
- Act with honesty, responsibility, and transparency.
- Respect approved architecture, security, quality, and governance decisions.
- Use official channels to raise concerns, disagreements, or proposals.
- Respect the confidentiality of information to which they have access.

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

Conduct that harms the working environment, security, or development of the project will not be tolerated. Such conduct includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, offensive, or humiliating comments.
- Insults, abusive language, or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorization.
- Impersonation or falsely claiming authorship of contributions.
- Deliberately manipulating discussions, reviews, or approval processes.
- Using project resources for illegal or unauthorized activities.
- Retaliating against individuals who report inappropriate conduct in good faith.
- Pressuring, harassing, or attempting to obtain contributions through threats or coercion.

Technical disagreements, well-founded criticism, and the rejection of proposals do not, by themselves, constitute inappropriate behavior, provided they are expressed respectfully.

## 5. Collaboration and Communication

Collaboration within School Guardian must follow the processes established in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the project's governance policies.

Participants are expected to:

- Keep project-related discussions within the appropriate channels.
- Avoid repetitive comments, provocation, or discussions that do not contribute to resolving issues.
- Support technical observations with verifiable criteria.
- Respect reasonable review and response times.
- Avoid presenting personal opinions as official project decisions.
- Not assume administrative, approval, or representation authority that has not been granted to them.

## 6. Respect for Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

All participants must respect intellectual property rights, access conditions, and confidentiality obligations applicable to School Guardian.

In particular:

- They must not claim contributions made by others as their own.
- They must acknowledge the authorship of others' work where appropriate.
- They must not disclose code, documentation, credentials, or internal information without authorization.
- They must comply with applicable contribution agreements and terms of use.
- They must not use information obtained through their participation to harm the project or third parties.

Acknowledgment of authorship does not, by itself, imply a transfer of economic rights or grant additional permissions over the software.

## 7. Enforcement Responsibilities

Enforcement of this code is the responsibility of individuals with administrative or maintenance responsibilities within the `school-guardian-project` organization, according to the permissions and authority assigned to them.

These individuals may:

- Receive reports of potential violations.
- Assess reported situations.
- Request clarification or additional information.
- Take proportionate measures to protect the collaborative environment.
- Escalate situations requiring further assessment.

Decisions must be made reasonably, impartially, and with consideration for the seriousness of the conduct.

## 8. Reporting Inappropriate Behavior

Individuals who witness or experience conduct that violates this code may report it to the project's authorized maintainers through the appropriate official channel.

**Official reporting channel:** Pending definition.

Reports should include, whenever possible:

- A description of the events.
- The approximate date or period when they occurred.
- The individuals or spaces involved.
- Available evidence that may help assess the situation.

Reports must be handled discreetly, with efforts made to protect the privacy of everyone involved.

Retaliation against individuals who submit reports in good faith or assist in assessing a situation will not be tolerated.

## 9. Measures for Violations

Depending on the nature, severity, and recurrence of the conduct, project maintainers may take measures such as:

- Formal warning.
- Request to correct or remove inappropriate content.
- Temporary restriction of participation in discussions or activities.
- Suspension of access permissions.
- Revocation of collaboration permissions.
- Removal of an individual from specific project activities.

Measures must be proportionate and may be applied immediately when there is a significant risk to the project's security, information, or integrity.

Where appropriate, the circumstances may be referred to the relevant legal or contractual authorities.

## 10. Implementation and Review

This code forms part of School Guardian's collaboration guidelines and must be known and respected by everyone participating in the project.

Its content may be updated when changes occur in the governance structure, collaboration processes, or project requirements.
